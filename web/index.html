    About this map
    Pink door panels mark the women's coach, at the front. Each line runs its own number of coaches; the car design and band colour are the same on every line. Close up, only the cars in view are drawn in full detail.
    Underground sections are drawn at their depth, seen through the map. Click a station, or search for one, to fly to it. Right-drag (on a phone, two fingers) to tilt and turn.
    Track and stations © OpenStreetMap contributors; station order and run times from DMRC. Viaduct height, tunnel depth, station buildings and cars are drawn to typical sizes; the livery is approximate.
